Berlin

Berlin (whose real name is Andrés 
de Fonollosa) is one of the most
iconic, complex, and polarizing
characters in the Netflix series
La Casa de Papel (Money Heist),
portrayed brilliantly by Spanish
actor Pedro Alonso.

He is a gem thief, a brilliant cracksman, and
the second-in-command during the Royal Mint of 
Spain heist. He also helped design the 
blueprints and conceptualize both the Mint 
heist and the subsequent Bank of Spain heist 
alongside The Professor and Palermo.
He views crime as an art form. He maintains 
elegance, composure, and a theatrical
demeanor even under extreme pressure.
While generally calm and refined, he exhibits 
sociopathic tendencies, lacking empathy when 
maintaining control or making difficult 
decisions.
