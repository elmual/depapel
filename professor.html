Profesor

1. Who is the Professor?

The Professor, whose real name is Sergio Marquina, is one of the main characters in the Spanish television series Money Heist, also known as La Casa de Papel.

He is the mastermind behind the heists carried out by his team. Unlike traditional criminals, the Professor does not rely on physical strength, violence, or intimidation. Instead, he uses intelligence, logic, psychology, patience, and strategic thinking to achieve his goals.

He is a highly intelligent and mysterious man who prefers to stay behind the scenes rather than be at the center of attention.

His greatest weapon is not a gun. It is his mind.


2. His personality

The Professor has a complex personality. He is calm, analytical, introverted, and extremely detail-oriented.

1. Intelligent

He can connect pieces of information that other people overlook. He often anticipates possible problems and develops solutions before they occur.

2. Strategic

He rarely acts without thinking. He considers different possibilities, predicts people's reactions, and prepares alternative plans.

3. Patient

The Professor understands that successful plans require time. He can spend months preparing for a single operation.

4. Introverted

He is not naturally comfortable with social situations. He sometimes struggles to express his feelings and communicate with people on an emotional level.

5. Observant

He pays attention to small details, facial expressions, body language, and changes in people's behavior. These observations help him understand what others might be thinking.

6. Secretive

He rarely reveals everything about himself. He prefers to control the information other people have about him.
